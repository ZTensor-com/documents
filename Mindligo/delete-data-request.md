# MindLigo Data Deletion Requests

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This page explains how MindLigo users can request deletion of their account and associated data, or request deletion of specific data without deleting their account.

MindLigo is operated by **ZTensor**.

## Delete Your MindLigo Account

You can request account deletion directly inside the MindLigo app.

### Steps

1. Open the **MindLigo** app.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Open **Settings**.
4. Tap **Delete Account**.
5. Type `DELETE` to confirm.
6. Tap **Request Account Deletion**.

After you submit the request:

- You are signed out immediately.
- Your account is marked as pending deletion and can no longer be used normally while the request is being processed.
- Our team begins processing the request within **7 business days**.

### What Data Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, we delete the account and associated personal data tied to it, including:

- Your member profile and candidate profile data
- Your partner preferences and match history
- Your connections and shortlists
- Your profile photos and uploaded media
- Your account credentials and related account records
- Your push notification token
- Your blocks, reports, and related safety records tied to your account

### What Data May Be Kept for Longer

Some data may be retained for limited periods after an account deletion request:

- **Profile and personal information:** deleted within **30 days**
- **Chat messages:** may be retained for up to **90 days** for safety and moderation purposes
- **Anonymized or aggregated data:** may be retained indefinitely because it is no longer linked to your identity

If we are required by law to retain specific records, those records may be kept for the period required by law.

## Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

MindLigo also allows you to request deletion of some data without deleting your account.

### How to Request Partial Data Deletion

Email **support@mindligo.com** with the subject line:

`Privacy Request`

In your email, include:

- Your MindLigo account email address
- Your name
- A clear description of the data you want deleted

Examples of data you can ask us to delete without deleting your account:

- Profile photos
- Optional profile fields
- Partner preference data
- Feedback screenshots or support attachments
- Push notification token

### Data That May Need To Be Kept If Your Account Stays Active

If you keep your account open, some core data may need to remain in order to provide the service, such as:

- Your account email address
- Basic account identifiers
- Records needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance

If deleting specific data would materially affect app functionality, we will let you know when we respond to your request.

## Response Time

We aim to respond to privacy and deletion requests within **30 days**.
