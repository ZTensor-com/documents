Child Safety Standards

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Our Commitment

MindLigo, operated by ZTensor, is a matrimonial matchmaking service intended exclusively for adults seeking marriage. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). The exploitation or endangerment of a minor in any form is strictly prohibited on our platform and is abhorrent to our mission.

These standards describe how we prevent, detect, and respond to CSAE, in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and applicable laws.

Adults Only — 18+ Service

MindLigo is restricted to users who are 18 years of age or older. The platform is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to create an account or use the service.

  • Every user must provide a date of birth and confirm they are at least 18 during registration.
  • Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are removed promptly.
  • We do not market, design, or position the app toward minors in any way.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited and result in immediate removal and account termination:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form — images, video, text, or links.
  • Sexualization of minors, including grooming, solicitation, or enticement of a child.
  • Any attempt to use the platform to contact, exploit, traffic, or endanger a minor.
  • Sharing, requesting, or promoting CSAE content or off-platform CSAE channels.
  • Misrepresenting age to access the service or to deceive other users.

Our Prevention and Detection Practices

  • Age gating: mandatory date-of-birth collection and an 18+ requirement at sign-up.
  • Trust-network model: introductions are made through verified personal connections rather than anonymous open browsing, reducing the risk of predatory anonymous contact.
  • In-app reporting and blocking: users can report and block any profile, message, or shared content directly within the app, including a dedicated "Child safety concern" report category.
  • Human review: reports of suspected CSAE are prioritized and reviewed by our team for swift action.
  • Enforcement: we remove violating content, terminate offending accounts, and preserve relevant evidence as required by law.

How to Report Child Safety Concerns

We provide in-app tools to report child safety concerns. To report a profile, message, or content, use the Report option available within the app and select "Child safety concern", or contact our child safety point of contact below. We review every report and act promptly.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement or emergency services right away, then notify us so we can assist and preserve evidence.

Reporting to Authorities and Legal Compliance

MindLigo complies with all relevant child safety laws and cooperates with regional and national authorities. When we become aware of apparent CSAM or CSAE, we remove the content, preserve relevant data, and report to the appropriate authorities, including the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, and to regional and national authorities in the relevant jurisdiction.
