# Request deletion of your Remo account.

Use the in-app flow when possible, or submit a request by email.

## Fastest option: request deletion in the app

1. Open Remo and sign in to your employee account.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Request account deletion**.
4. Read the confirmation and submit the request.

After the request succeeds, Remo signs you out and disables account access.

## Request deletion without signing in

If you cannot access the app, email [support@ztensor.com](mailto:support@ztensor.com?subject=Remo%20account%20deletion%20request) from the email address connected to your Remo account.

Include the following information:

* Your full name.
* Your Remo account email address.
* Your organization or employer name.
* Whether you used Remo as an employee, admin, or both.
* A short message saying you want your Remo account deleted.

## What happens after a deletion request

* Your Remo profile is deactivated and sign-in access is disabled.
* Detailed location pings are deleted.
* Device push tokens are disabled.
* Geofence assignments are removed.
* An audit event is retained to document the privacy request.

## Information that may be retained

Remo is an employer-managed workforce application. Employers may need to retain payroll, attendance, work session summaries, audit logs, tax, legal, or compliance records after account deletion. Detailed location pings are removed as part of the deletion flow, but summary records may remain where retention is required.

## Processing time

We aim to acknowledge external deletion requests within 7 days and complete eligible deletion actions within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for identity verification, legal compliance, security, or employer record obligations.

For privacy questions, email [support@ztensor.com](mailto:support@ztensor.com).
